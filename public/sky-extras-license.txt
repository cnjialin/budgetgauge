https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Images/2018/04/Gaia_s_sky_in_colour2

The source image is retained without editing. At runtime it is sampled using
its Hammer projection and transformed from Galactic coordinates to the local
sky. It is downsampled and dimmed for a diffuse background band. Any derived
texture is licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO.
