Adaptation: exclude the Sun, filter visual magnitude <= 7, retain J2000
right ascension (hours), declination (degrees), visual magnitude, B-V color
index and proper motion (mas/year). The derived real-sky-catalog.json and
its embedded copy in water-demo.html are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
The catalog is a stellar map, not a solar-system ephemeris.
